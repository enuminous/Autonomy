# EFMW as a testable operational proposal

This repository uses EFMW as Monolithic's proposed lens for tracking a system's expectations, observed effects and corrective response. It does not import an unspecified field equation as a proven governance mechanism. No physical interpretation, theorem of general stability, sentience detector or superiority claim follows from the implementation.

## Research loop

Observe authorized context → state an expectation → select a permitted action → obtain an externally held outcome record → compare → challenge and repair → replay the case.

For a measurable output channel j, a candidate residual is:

`r[j,t] = observed[j,t] - predicted[j,t]`

An output, unit, prediction horizon, missing-data rule and measurement source must be fixed before this has meaning. Categorical judgments require a declared scoring rule rather than arbitrary subtraction. Uncertainty and censoring must remain visible. These residuals are research variables, not rights scores.

Candidate channels include missed commitments, timing error, scope violations, disputed decisions, successful remedy latency and unexplained identity changes. Some are rare binary events; some are continuous. Keep them separate unless a preregistered aggregation is justified. A low average residual cannot compensate for one unauthorized disclosure.

## Hard constraints precede optimization

The reference policy returns DENY for known absent/revoked authority or a known rights conflict, HOLD for unresolved prerequisites and ELIGIBLE only when declared prerequisites are satisfied. Operator pause also holds execution. ELIGIBLE is permission to consider an action, not a command to perform it.

The policy's booleans are external findings supplied by an evaluator. The code cannot determine ownership, consent validity, reviewer independence or legal applicability. Those require named sources and integration controls.

## What an EFMW contribution would have to show

A baseline can already enforce authorization, keep signed receipts and route appeals. To establish additional value, compare that baseline with a precisely specified EFMW-enhanced policy on the same authorized cases and outcome definitions. Measure calibration, missed harm, unnecessary holds, remedy latency and operational cost at comparable risk thresholds. Reserve held-out cases and disclose uncertainty and adverse results.

AUT-001 presently tests the baseline evidence mechanics. It does not implement field equations or measure EFMW's incremental benefit. A future mathematical module must identify its source revision, equations, assumptions, dimensions, numerical method, failure conditions and falsifiable predictions before integration.

Do not retrofit favorable observations into a claimed prediction. A formal proof is limited to its definitions and assumptions; it does not establish the correspondence between a modeled reviewer and an independent real institution.
