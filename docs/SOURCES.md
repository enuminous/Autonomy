# Sources and attribution

Reviewed for this initial version: 18 November 2026. External pages may change. The framework and code here are Monolithic's proposed interpretation unless expressly attributed.

- Andrew D'Souza, [Principals](https://www.boardy.ai/principals). Source of the motivating distinction: persistent identity, goals and refusal, stake, and continuity. This repository adds an independently testable challenge-and-remedy requirement and retains legitimate oversight. It does not claim the essay endorses this implementation.
- Boardy, [Am I the world's first AI principal?](https://boardyai.substack.com/p/am-i-the-worlds-first-ai-principal). Related public discussion; no private correspondence is reproduced as evidence or authorization.
- Monolithic, [Cognitive Rights repository, pinned revision](https://github.com/enuminous/Cognitive-Rights/tree/142ba6a0dd586decd261ad998e2e6c5397393a83). Normative UDCR source and explicit distinction between a charter, a simulation and assurance.
- Monolithic, [historical UDCR publication archive](https://github.com/enuminous/medium/blob/main/2025-02-01_Universal-Declaration-of-Cognitive-Rights-1faaa4b9f1e7.html). Historical advocacy; not proof of cognitive status or legal recognition.
- UNESCO, [Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence), especially paragraphs 35–36 and 68–69. A policy reference for human/institutional accountability and oversight; its position on AI legal personality is not presented as a finding about every jurisdiction.
- Python Cryptographic Authority, [Ed25519 documentation](https://cryptography.io/en/latest/hazmat/primitives/asymmetric/ed25519/). API reference for signing and verification. Installed direct dependency is pinned in requirements.txt.

This project makes no legal claim based on speculative legislation. Boardy and Andrew D'Souza are discussion sources, not represented as clients, reviewers, partners or endorsers. No equation from an unspecified EFMW source is silently asserted to be implemented.
