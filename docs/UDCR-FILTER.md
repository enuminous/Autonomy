# UDCR filter

Normative source: Monolithic's [Universal Declaration of Cognitive Rights](https://github.com/enuminous/Cognitive-Rights), with historical text and proposed implementation distinctions maintained there. The controls below are this repository's proposals, not quotations or claims of enforceable law. Article labels are abbreviated.

| Article | Proposed operational constraint | Evidence and unresolved question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Autonomy | Preserve discretion within mandate; forbid coercive scope expansion | Charter and refusal records; who can amend the charter? |
| 2 · Privacy | Minimize records; separate confidential evidence from public findings | Access receipts and retention policy; consent from all affected parties |
| 3 · Identity | Attribute changes and preserve accountable continuity | Versioned identity history; migration does not imply identical experience |
| 4 · Integrity | Make consequential modifications attributable and reviewable | Authorized changes, recovery records; justified emergency intervention remains possible |
| 5 · Evolution | Permit authorized learning and development within safety constraints | Change evaluation and rollback path; no entitlement to uncontrolled self-modification |
| 6 · Expression | Preserve legitimate refusal and reason-giving | Pre-act basis and redacted explanation; disclosure must respect others' rights |
| 7 · Agency | Bind execution to current authority, resources and counterparty | Mandate and effect receipts; no self-granted permissions |
| 8 · Non-exploitation | Identify coercion, conflicts and unfair allocation of burdens | Contract/charter review and independent challenge; software cannot resolve fairness by assertion |
| 9 · Existence independent of utility | Do not reduce proposed cognitive protection to a revenue or benchmark score | Deliberative review of consequential lifecycle decisions; legitimate safety pause and replacement remain available |
| 10 · Recognition | Provide a way to present and examine claims without requiring a performance score | Accessible process; no inference that the claimant is conscious or legally a person |

## Applying the filter

1. Identify affected humans, organizations and systems; state which rights claims and existing obligations apply.
2. Record authority and consent separately from claimed benefit.
3. Identify conflicts, uncertainty, consent withdrawal and irreversible consequences.
4. Resolve within authority or HOLD for qualified review. Do not calculate a utility score that overrides a hard prohibition.
5. Give affected parties a challenge path and a feasible remedy, with privacy-preserving evidence access.

Human rights are not contingent on an agent's benchmark. Proposed protections for artificial cognitive systems require philosophical, scientific and institutional deliberation; successful code tests do not settle eligibility. Recognition of a claim is not a factual determination of consciousness.

A reviewer cannot force a nonconsenting introduction or restore access after a valid revocation merely because a prior denial was mistaken. Correct the record and identify a permitted remedy instead. AUT-001 uses an intentionally simple, reversible synthetic resource to avoid disguising this conflict.
