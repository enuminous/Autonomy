# Proposed pilot: one independently reviewable decision

Status: uncommissioned proposal. No Boardy integration or reviewer appointment is represented as agreed.

## Gate 0 · Authority and scope

Identify the accountable operator, rights holder, actual reviewer, custodian, executor and observer. Obtain accepted responsibilities, declared conflicts, access boundaries and a feasible remedy. Choose a low-risk, reversible decision class. Fix the criterion, authorized counterparty, dates, resource and evaluation period. Determine whether independent challenge is required; do not let the assessed operator choose N/A by omission.

## Gate 1 · Independent route

Inspect who appoints, pays, removes and can overrule the reviewer; who controls evidence and keys; and whether the reviewer can direct a valid correction without the original decider's permission. Test challenge delivery and evidence access. Where independence or authority is unresolved, report that finding before testing live effects.

## Gate 2 · Authorized exercise

Use an existing legitimate appeal, or a clearly labeled authorized exercise involving harmless synthetic content. Record basis before action and deposit it with the custodian. Preserve the mandate. Observe challenge submission, reasoned determination, current consent recheck, scoped execution and endpoint readback. Do not manufacture a harmful denial or demand an unjustified reversal.

A synthetic exercise in real infrastructure remains an exercise. A real challenged decision requires its own evidence. Separate the categories in the report.

## Gate 3 · Report and challenge

Report claim accuracy, route/control findings and remedy findings separately. Include case/version, sampling, evidence access, remaining harm, conflicts and exclusions. Give the assessed party a factual correction route without allowing it to suppress adverse findings. Publish only cleared material. No investment figures, private correspondence or customer records enter this public repository by default.

## Optional EFMW comparison

Only after the control baseline works, preregister a concrete enhanced policy and matched baseline. Freeze test cases and thresholds before evaluation, preserve held-out cases and define handling of missing observations. Measure false permissions, unnecessary holds, review latency, successful remedies, calibration and cost. Report uncertainty, unsuccessful cases and material operational differences. Do not infer benefit from a single favorable reversal.

## Acceptance and stop conditions

A narrow pass requires all applicable criteria and independently accessible evidence for the particular tested claim. Stop effects on withdrawn authority, rights conflict, unexpected disclosure, uncertain scope, compromised keys or loss of the agreed reviewer. Retain only permitted evidence and document the unresolved result. Expansion to additional counterparties, deployment powers or data classes requires a new scoped authorization.
