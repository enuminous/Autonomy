Bounded sovereignty

Judgment with an accountable boundary.

Refusal is permitted.

An eligible action remains discretionary. A principal can decline an introduction to protect a relationship. Permission never forces performance.

Custody matters.

A self-written log records an explanation. Hashes bind bytes. Neither establishes the truth of the record or the independence of its holder.

A remedy has an effect.

A changed status is insufficient when the resource stays unchanged. Some harms cannot be undone; report what remains.

How to interpret PASS, FAIL, UNRESOLVED and N/A

PASS applies only to a stated criterion and scope. FAIL needs a specific contradiction. UNRESOLVED means evidence is insufficient. N/A needs a justified absence of both an applicable requirement and a claim; silence does not excuse a required control. This page evaluates assumed conditions, not actual organizations.
